Biosculpter Detox [1.6]

[h1]Biosculpter Detox[/h1]

Curing an addiction usually means locking a colonist away and waiting out the withdrawal. Biosculpter Detox adds a detox cycle to the biosculpter pod that clears the addiction and the tolerance sitting behind it.

[h2]CORE FEATURES[/h2]
[list]
[*] Adds a detox cycle to every biosculpter pod
[*] Cures drug addictions, including ones added by other mods
[*] Clears drug tolerances at the same time, so the next dose starts from nothing
[*] Sends a letter naming the colonist when the cycle finishes
[/list]

[h2]HOW IT WORKS[/h2]
[list]
[*] Becomes available once you have researched bioregeneration
[*] Put the colonist in the pod and choose the detox cycle
[*] The cycle takes 12 days by default, and you can set it anywhere from 1 to 30 days in the mod's options
[*] For comparison, age reversal takes 8 days and bioregeneration 25
[*] A cycle already running keeps the length it started with
[*] The colonist comes out with their addictions and tolerances gone
[/list]

[h2]WHAT IT LEAVES ALONE[/h2]
[list]
[*] Permanent addictions are left alone unless you turn on "Treat permanent addictions" in the options, which is off by default
[*] Luciferium is the only permanent addiction the base game has
[*] Injuries, missing parts and anything that is not a drug dependency are untouched
[/list]

[h2]REQUIREMENTS[/h2]
[list]
[*] Ideology, which is the expansion the biosculpter pod comes from
[*] Harmony
[*] Can be added to a colony already in progress
[*] Finish or cancel a running detox cycle before removing the mod
[/list]
